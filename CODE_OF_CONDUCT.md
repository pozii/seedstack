# Code of Conduct

Seedstack follows the Contributor Covenant. The short version: be kind, be direct about technical problems without making them personal, and assume good intent until proven otherwise.

## Our standards

Examples of welcome behavior include using inclusive language, respecting different viewpoints, accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unwelcome behavior include harassment in any form, insulting or derogatory remarks, publishing private information without consent, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for serious or repeated violations. Reports go to the maintainers through a private channel on the repository, and every report is reviewed with discretion.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
